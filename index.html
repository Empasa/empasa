Method

How we engineer AI

Context and harness engineering

The model is the small part. We design what it is given to read, which tools it may call and in what order, and the automated checks that every answer must pass before a person sees it.

Workflows with a human decision

The AI investigates and proposes. A person approves. Every approved change is recorded with the value it replaced, and can be undone. Nobody has to trust a black box.

Data governance

Each customer’s data stays inside its own boundary. Credentials are encrypted, data is stored in the European Union, prompts are not used to train models, and every action leaves an audit record.

The same discipline goes into how we build: measured guardrails in the codebase, evidence before a claim, and verification on the running system before anything is called done.
