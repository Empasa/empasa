The booking that vanished was in a second account

A member told a marketplace owner that her upcoming booking and all her messages had disappeared. The owner logged in as the member and saw an empty inbox. The question that reached us: did the last update delete data?

Nothing was deleted

The member had two accounts. The first was years old, carried over from the marketplace’s previous website, with no active plan and no bookings ever. The second was a few months old, with a paid plan, a listing, a confirmed booking and the full message thread.

That morning she had signed in to the old account. The owner then opened that same account to check, and confirmed what the member saw. Both were looking at a real, empty account.

The signs were in the screenshots

  • The account the owner opened showed zero transactions. A member with a confirmed booking cannot have zero.
  • The property address was written differently in the member’s screenshot and in the other party’s screenshot. Two spellings meant two listings, written by two accounts.
  • The email on the empty account was not the email on the booking.

Why the update could not be the cause

The last change to the booking happened months before the last software release, and the platform’s event history held no deletion events. An update cannot remove something and leave no trace of the removal.

What to do first next time

  1. Search for every account with that person’s name, before looking at logs or releases.
  2. List the bookings of each account. The data is almost always in the other one.
  3. Compare emails, addresses and display names across the screenshots you were sent. A mismatch means different records.
  4. Answer with the two email addresses and one instruction: sign in with the other one.

We ran this case through our support investigation tool afterwards. It returned both accounts, the booking and the cause in about two minutes. “My data disappeared” is usually a question about identity, and the accounts answer it faster than the logs.
